Moves
    Rules in brief
    • All 15 checkers start on your 24-point; you move to 1 and bear off past it. The bot starts on your 1-point and moves the other way.
    • Landing on a lone enemy checker pins it (محبوس) until all your checkers leave that point. A pin or two checkers block a point.
    • Pinning the opponent's last checker on its starting point (the mother) wins at once, double.
    • Bear off only when all 15 are home and none is pinned. A win is 1 point, or 2 (mars) if the loser has borne off nothing.
    • Use both dice if you can; doubles play four times. The winner of a game starts the next one.
